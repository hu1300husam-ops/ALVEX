- The photos in this kit are AI-generated **concept visuals**, cropped from generated reference collages. They are not authentic evidence of ALVEX operations, employees, offices, partner facilities, Canton Fair attendance, or identifiable landmarks.
- Do not publish the image with fictional ALVEX team uniforms, logos, buildings, endorsements or other apparent company proofs as documentary or factual images.
- Some concepts include inaccurate or AI-generated signage and Chinese text. Inspect each photo before publishing; replace them with approved company photography when available.
- These are **low-to-medium resolution design placeholders** for prototyping, NOT a licensed authentic high-resolution production photography set. The hero image particularly needs a true high-resolution approved photo or video before launch.
- The original ALVEX wordmark and monogram PNGs are rasterized from the USER-PROVIDED official logo PDF. Use these instead of generated collage logos.
- Abstract SVGs are original decorative assets; safe to use as backgrounds and subtle textures.
- All photo crops intentionally omit the reference labels where possible, though visually inspect for artifacts.
