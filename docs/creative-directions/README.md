## Direction A — "The Ledger" (Editorial Magazine Spread)

- **Editorial layout:** A 12-column print grid with a visible hairline "ledger" rule system. Sections are numbered like the chapters of a business quarterly (01 / 02 …), with folios in the margins.
- **Hero composition:** Oversized two-line headline set across the full measure, with a narrow portrait-format image cropped into the right column and a caption block ("Image placeholder — approved Yiwu market photography to be supplied"). The hero is ~88vh, never taller than the viewport.
- **Typographic personality:** Confident grotesk display (Manrope 700, tight tracking, 96–112px) paired with a quieter body. Figures and numbers use tabular setting, like financial tables.
- **Visual rhythm:** Long text columns alternate with single full-bleed images. Each section starts with a rule and a folio, which gives the page a steady, publication-like pace.
- **Interaction concept:** Scroll-linked "page turn": sections reveal with a short mask wipe. Hovering a service name in the index expands its description in the margin.
- **Strength:** Most credible for a B2B audience; reads as a serious advisory publication.
- **Risk:** Can feel static and text-heavy if images are missing; relies on strong photography.

## Direction B — "The Gallery of Trade" (Horizontal Plate Sequence)

- **Editorial layout:** A sequence of large framed "plates" (like an exhibition catalogue) laid out on an asymmetric grid, with captions treated as wall labels.
- **Hero composition:** Headline floats on a deep-navy field; a single wide panoramic plate slides in beneath it. The plate is the only moving image.
- **Typographic personality:** Light-to-medium display with generous size contrast; small uppercase catalogue labels ("PLATE 03 — SUPPLIER MEETING, YIWU").
- **Visual rhythm:** Big–small–big plate pacing, with varied aspect ratios (4:5, 16:9, 1:1) so no two sections share a silhouette.
- **Interaction concept:** Drag-free horizontal "plate strip" on desktop; on mobile it becomes a native swipe carousel with snap points.
- **Strength:** Most memorable and cinematic; strong for "Real Operations".
- **Risk:** Horizontal scrolling and carousels are harder to read for long-form B2B content, and the concept depends heavily on high-quality photography.

## Direction C — "Field Notes" (Operational Dossier)

- **Editorial layout:** A two-tier layout: a fixed-width "dossier index" on the left (sticky on desktop) and content on the right. Sections are written like dated field notes with quiet metadata.
- **Hero composition:** Full-width navy hero with the headline on the left and a small, precise "Where we work" panel showing the single confirmed location (Yiwu, China) and the operating scope in plain words.
- **Typographic personality:** Editorial serif display for headlines paired with a neutral sans for body and labels. This is the most distinctive typographic voice of the three, but the least conventional.
- **Visual rhythm:** Dense information zones alternate with breathing space; works well for the process and Canton Fair sections.
- **Interaction concept:** A sticky index that highlights the current section, and a "stage" for the sourcing journey that updates as the reader scrolls.
- **Strength:** Strongest for informational clarity and operational credibility.
- **Risk:** The serif-led identity may feel less "global" to some buyers; the sticky index can look like a documentation site.

### Selection: Direction A — "The Ledger"

**Why:**
1. It matches the brief's 70% premium global editorial mix most closely, and it communicates a business advisory identity rather than a freight brochure.
2. It is the only direction that remains strong while photography is still pending, which is the actual situation with this materials set. Gallery depends on images the company has not yet supplied.
3. Its mobile composition is simple and reliable: single column, large type, numbered sections, and no carousels, which suits B2B readers on phones.
4. Its motion (mask reveals, margin expansion) is restrained and easy to make reduced-motion-safe.
5. It keeps ALVEX's real information (Yiwu, services, Canton Fair, contact channels) readable and prominent.

**What we borrow:** From Direction C, the "Where we work" fact panel and the sticky chapter index on desktop. From Direction B, one large cinematic plate for the Operations section once real photography is supplied.
